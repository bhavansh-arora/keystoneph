Does it cost me anything?

No. Keystone pays for studies, development, construction and operations. Landowners receive lease income and utilities receive cost-assured energy.

How much land do I need?

Utility-scale projects generally start around 20 acres, with roughly 5 to 7 acres per megawatt. Smaller parcels may suit distributed or community projects, which we also develop.

What makes a site a strong candidate?

Flat, open, cleared land close to three-phase power lines or a substation with capacity is ideal. Brownfields and capped landfills can also be excellent sites.

What happens at the end of the lease?

The agreement sets out end-of-term options before you sign, including removal of the equipment and restoration of the land.

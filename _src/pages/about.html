(01) Our story

Our story

Keystone Power Holdings is a renewable energy company that develops, finances and owns solar facilities across North America. We help clients realize savings through the production of clean energy while reducing their carbon footprint.

We specialize in medium to large scale photovoltaic systems, ground-mounted or rooftop, built on or near our clients' property. We pass the savings straight on to them.

We believe the country's energy demands, and its energy costs, will keep rising. So we built a model that lets municipalities, schools, non-profits and businesses capture the benefits of clean energy without the up-front capital investment that usually stands in the way.

Behind every project is a network of partners we've chosen carefully, from our affiliate KPH Construction Services to the banks and tax-equity investors who fund our work.

(02) Founders

Two careers in solar. One company.

Between them, Keystone's co-founders have run a billion-dollar solar business, developed 50+ MW of distributed generation and founded several companies.

{{> founder-anthony}}

Partner management, due diligence, deal placement & financing

Anthony was President and CEO of Conergy Americas, responsible for the North, Central and South American business units of a billion-dollar global solar company, and sat on the global Conergy board. He joined Conergy in 2006 in corporate strategy and sales, then led distribution and served as CFO/COO for the Americas before taking the top role.

His teams sold, built and/or financed more than 300 MW of solar PV. Earlier, he co-founded Photon Consulting, one of the first solar market research and consulting firms, focused on demand forecasting, value-chain price analysis and emerging technologies.

Before solar, he spent over a decade in operations and consulting with Ernst & Young and Accenture across renewable energy, consumer products, high tech, retail and transportation. He holds an MBA from MIT and a BS in Computer Science and Decision Sciences.

{{> founder-william}}

Development pipeline, land & deal acquisition

William leads Keystone's development pipeline, land development and deal acquisition across North America. He has developed and built commercial solar arrays in several US states and in Puerto Rico.

In 2011 he founded Green Energy Partners, developing distributed solar through power purchase agreements with municipalities, schools and universities, and has developed and built over 50 MW of distributed generation. Before that, as part of Conergy's senior development team, he delivered over 15 MW in under a year.

An entrepreneur by background, he founded national telecom staffing firm TMI FirstSearch in 1994, grew it to multimillion-dollar revenue and 55+ employees, and sold it to Berwyn Consulting Group in 2006. He also owned and ran WRD Properties, acquiring and operating residential and commercial buildings in Pennsylvania and New Jersey. He attended Villanova University and Stockton State College.

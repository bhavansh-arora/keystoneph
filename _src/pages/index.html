(01) What we do

We turn idle roofs, open land and parking lots into power plants that cut your electricity bill for decades. We fund the build. We own the system. You keep the savings.

01 {{icon:search}}

Develop

Site analysis, utility interconnection, permits, incentives and land rights, handled by a team that has developed solar in states across the US and in Puerto Rico.

02 {{icon:dollar}}

Finance

We bring the capital through our own tax-equity fund and banking partners, so your budget isn't touched. Flexible structures for organizations other financiers turn away.

03 {{icon:hammer}}

Build

Construction oversight by our affiliate, KPH Construction Services, working with experienced EPC and electrical partners from groundbreaking to permission to operate.

04 {{icon:shield}}

Own & operate

Keystone owns, insures and maintains the system for the life of the agreement. You buy the power it makes, for less than you pay the utility today.

(05) How it works

From bill to build in four steps.

You make a handful of decisions. We handle the engineering, capital, construction and upkeep.

See financing models{{icon:arrow}}
  1. 01

    Free feasibility review

    Share 12 months of utility bills and your site. We model system size, production, available incentives and an indicative rate, then show you the savings side by side with what you pay now.

    {{icon:clock}} Free, no obligation

  2. 02

    Agreement, not an invoice

    Choose a power purchase agreement, an operating lease or a utility-scale service agreement. You lock in a rate below the utility's, with no capital outlay and no maintenance liability.

    {{icon:file}} PPA, lease or service agreement

  3. 03

    Engineer, permit & build

    We secure interconnection and permits, fund construction and manage the build through KPH Construction Services, keeping disruption to your operations to a minimum.

    {{icon:hammer}} Rooftop, ground-mount or canopy

  4. 04

    Switch on & save for decades

    From permission to operate, you buy clean power at a discount. Keystone monitors, maintains and insures the system for the full term, so you get predictable energy costs year after year.

    {{icon:sun}} 20 to 30+ year term
